DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

Coaster Technologies, Inc.

Version 1.0  ·  Last updated October 29, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into between Coaster Technologies, Inc. ("Coaster") and the customer identified in the signature block or the applicable agreement ("Customer"), and forms part of and is incorporated into the Coaster Terms of Service or other written agreement between the parties governing Customer’s use of the Coaster application and related services (the "Agreement"). It applies to Coaster’s processing of Personal Information on Customer’s behalf. For the processing of Personal Information, this DPA controls over both the Agreement (including the Terms of Service) and the Privacy Policy; a separate signed customer agreement controls over all of these documents. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.

1. Definitions

"Personal Information" means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that Coaster processes on Customer’s behalf in providing the Service. "Customer Content" means the documents and information Customer submits to the Service (also referred to as "Your Content" in the Terms). "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Information. "Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Coaster to process Personal Information. "Data Protection Laws" means the privacy and data-protection laws applicable to the processing under this DPA.

2. Roles and Instructions

As between the parties, Customer is the controller (or, where Customer acts on behalf of its own clients, the processor) of Personal Information, and Coaster is the processor acting on Customer’s behalf. Coaster shall process Personal Information only to provide and support the Service and on Customer’s documented instructions, including as set out in the Agreement, this DPA, and Exhibit A, unless required by law (in which case Coaster will, where legally permitted, notify Customer first). Coaster will inform Customer if it believes an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3. Limitations on Use; No Sale; No Model Training

Coaster shall not: (a) sell or share Personal Information; (b) retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than providing the Service or as permitted by Data Protection Laws; (c) combine Personal Information with data from other sources except as needed to provide the Service; or (d) use Personal Information or Customer Content to train, fine-tune, or improve any machine-learning model, except with Customer’s written consent. Coaster certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions.

4. Confidentiality and Personnel

Coaster shall treat Personal Information as confidential and ensure that personnel authorized to process it are bound by written confidentiality obligations and have received appropriate training. Access is limited to personnel who need it to provide the Service.

5. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes Coaster to engage Subprocessors to process Personal Information to provide the Service, provided each Subprocessor is bound by written obligations no less protective than this DPA, and Coaster remains responsible for its Subprocessors’ performance. Coaster’s current Subprocessors — including its cloud-hosting and infrastructure provider and its AI-inference provider — are identified in Coaster’s subprocessor list, which Coaster will provide on request and keep current. Coaster will give Customer reasonable prior notice of any new Subprocessor so Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. If Customer reasonably objects and Coaster cannot provide the Service without the new Subprocessor, Customer may terminate the affected portion of the Service and receive a refund of any prepaid fees for the terminated portion.

6. Security

Coaster shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, loss, disclosure, or alteration, including encryption of Personal Information in transit and at rest, access controls and least-privilege access, support for multi-factor authentication, logging and monitoring, and periodic review of its safeguards.

7. Security Incident Notification

Coaster shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a confirmed or reasonably suspected breach of security leading to the unauthorized access to, or loss, alteration, or disclosure of, Personal Information (a "Security Incident"). The notice will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the incident, the information affected, and the measures taken, and Coaster will preserve relevant evidence, cooperate with Customer’s reasonable investigation, and take reasonable steps to mitigate the incident. Coaster’s notification is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

Liability for breach of this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability in the Agreement, except that the parties may agree in a signed customer agreement to a different (including higher) limit for breaches of this DPA or for Security Incidents. [For enterprise and public-sector deals, confirm the breach-liability figure you are prepared to accept.]

8. Assistance to Customer

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Coaster shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in: responding to requests from individuals to exercise their rights under Data Protection Laws that Customer cannot address through the Service’s own features; conducting data-protection impact assessments; and responding to inquiries from regulators, in each case where required by Data Protection Laws.

9. Audit

Coaster shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA, including, where available, third-party audit reports or certifications. Where those are insufficient to meet a requirement of Data Protection Laws, Customer may conduct, at its own expense and no more than once per year (absent a Security Incident or regulator requirement), an audit of Coaster’s relevant practices on at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice, during business hours, subject to confidentiality and without unreasonably disrupting Coaster’s operations.

10. Return and Deletion

On expiration or termination of the Agreement, or earlier on Customer’s written request, Coaster shall delete or, at Customer’s election, return Personal Information and Customer Content, and delete existing copies, within thirty (30) days (and from routine backups in the ordinary course of backup rotation), except to the extent retention is required by law or for de-identified, aggregated data that does not identify Customer, any individual, or any project. Coaster will certify deletion in writing on Customer’s request.

11. International Transfers

Coaster primarily processes Personal Information in the United States. Coaster’s development and technical personnel and providers do not have standing access to production Personal Information or production Customer Content; where any such access is necessary, it occurs only as a Subprocessor under this DPA, under written confidentiality obligations, and limited to what is required. Where Coaster or a Subprocessor processes Personal Information outside the jurisdiction in which it was collected, the parties shall ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards as required by applicable Data Protection Laws, including standard contractual clauses where applicable.

12. Public-Sector Customers

Where Customer is a governmental or public-sector entity, the parties acknowledge that Customer Content and records may be subject to the Texas Public Information Act and similar public-records and retention laws, and that Customer remains responsible for meeting its own statutory obligations. Nothing in this DPA requires either party to act inconsistently with applicable public-records law.

13. Term; General

This DPA takes effect when the Agreement does and remains in effect for as long as Coaster processes Personal Information on Customer’s behalf. It is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, consistent with the Agreement. Except as modified here, the Agreement remains in full force. This DPA may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.
