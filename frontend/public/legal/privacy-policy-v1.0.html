Information you provide: your name, email address, job title, organization, phone number, profile image, and account preferences, collected when you request a demo, create an account (including through a third-party sign-in such as Google or GitHub), contact us, or register for a mailing list or event.

Your Content: the documents and information you submit for analysis. We handle Your Content only as described in the Terms — to provide and support the Service to you — and not to train any AI model without your consent.

We use personal information to: provide, operate, secure, and support the Service; create and manage accounts and authenticate users; communicate with you, including service and administrative messages; process payments; understand and improve how the Service is used; comply with legal obligations; and protect the rights, safety, and property of Coaster, our users, and others. The Service uses artificial intelligence to analyze documents and generate flags for professional review. AI-generated output may be inaccurate or incomplete and must be independently reviewed before you rely on it.
